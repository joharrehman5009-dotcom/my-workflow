FAQ

Got any questions?

How long before I see results?

Technical fixes can show movement in weeks; content and authority take months. Most projects show measurable change in Search Console within 6 to 12 weeks, with the larger gains compounding after that. Anyone promising page 1 in 30 days is selling something.

Do you work on paid ads as well as SEO?

Yes, and I prefer to run them together. Paid data tells you which keywords actually convert, which makes the organic strategy far sharper. Treating them as two separate jobs wastes the most useful signal you have.

What does an SEO audit actually cover?

A full crawl of the site, then technical, on-page and content review, plus keyword and competitor gap analysis. You get a prioritised fix list ordered by impact rather than by ease, and a call to walk through it.

Can you work with my existing site?

That is most of what I do. Whether it needs a technical cleanup, a content structure, schema, or a full migration handled without losing rankings, I can step into an existing build rather than starting over.

Which platforms have you worked with?

WordPress, Shopify, Magento and Strapi among others, across e-commerce, travel, B2B services and agency portfolios. The platform changes what is easy; it rarely changes what actually needs fixing.

Do you handle local SEO?

Yes. Google Business Profile optimisation, local pack visibility, and the service and location pages that turn nearby searches into enquiries. It is often the fastest win for service businesses.

How do you report on progress?

Search Console and analytics data rather than screenshots of rankings. You see impressions, clicks, positions and conversions, plus what changed and what it moved. If something is not working I will tell you.

Not sure what your site needs?

Reach out and describe the situation. Most audits and strategy calls start within 48 hours, and you will get an honest read on what is holding the site back, with no sales pitch attached.
